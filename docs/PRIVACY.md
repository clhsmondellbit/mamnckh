# Privacy and data minimization

MẦM permits guest chat and does not require a real name for an ordinary conversation. It must not request passwords, OTPs, financial information, social-media credentials, unnecessary exact addresses, or unnecessary identifying details.

The MVP does not persist guest-chat transcripts. The optional support-request workflow is voluntary, consent-based, role protected, and limited to the form fields the student submits. It deliberately excludes the full chat transcript.

Schools must publish and review their own retention period, approved staff roles, lawful basis/consent process, incident procedure, and access policy before real deployment. The application does not claim legal compliance by itself.
